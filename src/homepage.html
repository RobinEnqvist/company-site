04 / The handover

You keep the machine. You don't keep us.

The build is a fixed project with a fixed price. After that you pay a license while you use the system — it stays maintained, patched and improved. Cancel whenever you like. Nothing is held hostage, because a machine you can't walk away from isn't a machine you own.

That's not generosity. It's the only arrangement that forces us to build something that actually works.
