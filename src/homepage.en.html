The problem

Paid social needs volume. Nobody has an engine for it.

Performance agencies are turning down paying clients today. Not because the client is wrong – the product is good, the brand strong, the team smart – but because nobody can produce ads at the pace the account demands.

The agency can analyse, steer and say exactly what needs doing. But the agency doesn't want to be the creative engine. The client can't do it. And five approved ads a quarter isn't enough anymore.

That's the gap Wers fills.

Different clients

Does it work when the clients aren't alike?

Yes. That's what the system is built for.

Every client has its own identity in Wers: tone, rules, what can and can't be said, what's been tested before and what won. Ten clients are ten briefs and ten logs – not ten times the work.

What grates in ordinary production is that every client is a new project from zero. With us, every client is a configuration, and every batch builds on the last.

What you get

What you get – and don't

You get

  • 20 video ads per batch, two batches a month
  • One report per batch that answers your hypothesis with the account's data
  • The brief format, so you can order right from day one
  • Approval before publishing, always

You don't get

  • Media buying. That's yours.
  • Another agency. We sit behind you, not beside you.

The brief format

How to order a batch

One page. Hypothesis, what you want to learn, angle, audience, format, hooks, what you already know, offer, and how the batch is measured.

If the hypothesis or the metric is missing, the brief comes back. Without them the batch can't be evaluated – and then it's just content.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you a production company?

No. A production company delivers files. We deliver the answer to the question you asked, and the next question.

Are you an AI tool?

No. We use them – several, and we switch when better ones come along. You don't need to learn any tool. You write a brief.

What if the batch performs badly?

Then it has answered the hypothesis. A batch that shows an angle doesn't work is a successful batch – you were spared three weeks polishing it.

Who owns the material?

Your client. Always. We ask for the right to show anonymised results as cases, but that's agreed separately.

Do you need access to the ad account?

Read access, yes. Without account data there's no report and no next hypothesis.

Which channels?

Meta and TikTok. Short video. Nothing else right now.

Which clients are a fit?

E-commerce with an ongoing paid social budget. Other industries we take on as we learn them – ask.
